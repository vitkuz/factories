# Lessons from turning quotes into questions

Read these before generating, judging or compacting questions. Each lesson is one line: what went wrong, and what to do instead. They are prior evidence from past runs, not law: when a lesson and a step's own rules disagree, the step's rules win. The last step of every run appends new ones at the end.

- Compression stripped the particle "ли" and turned questions into statements with "?"; keep "ли", "разве", "неужели" in every shortened version, they are not filler.
- The core's `quoteExplainsWhy` detects only explicit causal markers, so a quote that explains why through an analogy or an implied structure gets false; when the quote's whole purpose is to explain why, set the flag true in the core rather than leaving the judges to override it.
- The filter varies between strict and lenient on "Почему" questions from run to run; do not add rules for that, the judge's why/how rules normalise the outcome.
- For a plain assertion quote, the mirror-form literal question with "ли" ("Есть ли в политике друзья?") usually beats why/how framings; generate every literal pivot and let it compete.
- Mandatory archetype questions (why, how) often look wrong at generation time; generate them anyway and let the judges reject them, never skip them early.
- Scoring fields came back as quoted strings ("0.8"); write bare numbers.
- Do not add new archetype enums, container questions or structural rules on a hunch; change a rule only when a reviewed run shows the regression.
- The same question was generated by both the obvious and the scene branch and kept in both lists after filtering; deduplicate identical questions across branches at merge or filter and keep one copy.
- Near-paraphrases from different branches ("Что такое X?" and "Что такое X на самом деле?", "Можно ли добиться всего, чего хочешь?" and "Можно ли добиться всего и сразу?") all survived the filter as separate entries; at merge or filter treat questions with the same meaning and the same answer as duplicates and keep the best-scored one.
- The scene writer passed its own self-check while emitting questions that break the form rule (no "ли", "разве", "неужели" or fronted question word: "Может, это всё ошибка?", "Дед, ты правда не боишься?") or never name the quote's subject ("Есть ли надежда, что обойдётся?", "Почему он?"); check form and subject word by word on every scene question and rewrite those that fail, before writing the list.
- The scene branch wrote about half of its questions as why/how ("Как жить...", "Как принять...", "Почему все избегают...") although `quoteExplainsWhy`, `quoteIsReason`, `quoteExplainsHow` and `quoteIsPrinciple` were all false, and every one was judged weak; when those flags are false, write scene questions as yes/no ("ли") or "what is" questions that the quote answers directly.
- One generation list held many near-paraphrases of the same question (five or more ways to ask "how to recognise X" / "what is typical for X"), and the filter later dropped over half of the judged-direct questions as duplicates; when writing a list, give each distinct meaning one wording (the best one) instead of rephrasing it, and let the filter only remove, never reword.
- The author's underlying question, typed `inferred`, was copied verbatim into the history list as a long two-part question and judged weak; for an `inferred` question write a short one-part everyday version the quote answers directly, or skip it.
- Scene questions leaned on the scene with demonstratives ("такого", "такое", "такой" + noun) and were judged weak because the question alone points at something not in the quote; write each scene question so it stands alone, with the subject named in full and no demonstrative that needs the dialogue to be understood.
- The quote only said what something is not (a negative definition), yet the generators wrote positive-definition questions ("Что такое настоящая X?", "Что характерно для настоящей X?", "Какое главное правило в X?") and all were judged weak because the quote never says what X is; when the quote defines only by negation, write questions about the negated case ("Это ли X, если ...?", "Как понять, что это уже не X?") and do not ask for the positive definition.
- Dialog scenes were set in domains the core lists as forbidden analogies (work for money, selling, commissions) and their hidden questions were all judged weak with that domain named as the reason; before writing a scene check its setting and hidden question against `forbiddenAnalogyDomains` and pick a scene from `directQuestionDomains` or `lifeSituationsWhereItResonates` instead.
- Every hidden question of the dialog scenes was judged weak because it asked about the speaker's own grievance or entitlement (waiting, owing, deserving, who outplays whom, what a relative thinks) and not about the quote's central subject, so the quote could not answer it without invented facts; write each real question (`realQuestions`) about the central subject itself and check that the quote answers it in one sentence using only its own words.
- A history question was built from a concept that appears in the original work's context but not in the quote's own text (a notion of law or justice the quote never mentions) and was judged weak; build history questions only from words and ideas present in the quote, and take the context only to choose which of them to ask.
- The author's underlying question, typed `explicit`, was also copied verbatim into the history list as a long two-part question about a broader concept than the quote and judged weak; shorten it to one part the quote answers directly, or skip it, whatever the type.
- Scene questions swapped the quote's central subject for a looser near-synonym ("уважение", "признание", "почёт" instead of the quote's own word) and several were judged weak because the quote speaks only of its own word; write every scene question with the exact central subject word from the quote.
- Scene questions were built as dilemmas from the core's `centralTension` and `impliedConflicts` ("что важнее: X или Y", "можно ли быть хорошим, если ...") with a rival item the quote never names, and almost all were judged weak; the quote ranks its subject only against the class it names, so pit the subject against members of that class, or ask about the subject itself, and do not take a dilemma from the core unless the quote's own words state it.
- Obvious questions used a question word the quote does not supply ("Где", "Откуда берётся", "Как добиться", "Чего ожидать от тех, кто") when the quote only names a purpose, and all were judged weak; when the quote gives a purpose, ask with "зачем", "ради чего" or a "ли" form, and use "где", "откуда", "как", "чего ожидать" only if the quote itself names a place, source, method or outcome.
- Scene questions were built around side concepts taken from the core's `themes`, `impliedConflicts` and `likelyListenerStates` (fear, recklessness, caution, fearlessness, motive) that the quote itself never mentions, and every one was judged weak because the listener had to supply the missing step; a word being in the core is not enough, so ask only about concepts whose words stand in the quote text.
- Scene questions were built from props and roles of the dialog itself (an order, a document, a duty, a boss, a bystander's guilt) that the quote never mentions, and nearly all were judged weak; the scene can carry such details, but the real question must be about the quote's own subject and use only words from the quote text.
- Both history questions already contained the quote's answer in their wording ("what is worst when an unjust one gets arms") or asked "чем опасна" about a quote that only ranks its subject, and both were judged weak; write a history question so that the quote's claim is the reply and not part of the question.
- The obvious list repeated one superlative question across domains from the core ("во власти", "в политике", "в государстве", "на войне"), and all four survived as separate entries with the same answer; one domain-swept wording is enough, and the rest are duplicates to drop at merge or filter.
- The filter dropped judged-direct questions that were not duplicates ("Как понять, что речь хорошая?" next to "Что нужно, чтобы речь была хорошей?", "Каким должен быть слог оратора?" next to a style question) while its rules say to keep every `direct`; remove a direct question only when another kept one has the same meaning and the same answer, and keep the one with the higher score.
- The core set `quoteIsDefinition` true (with a `definedConcept`) for an "X is the result of Y" sentence that names a cause, and the obvious branch then asked "Что такое X?", which the judges rejected because the quote says where X comes from, not what it is; set `quoteIsDefinition` true only when the quote says what X is in its own right, and for a causal "X is the result of Y" ask "откуда", "от чего зависит" or a "ли" form instead.
- Obvious questions turned a superlative claim ("X does Y better than anyone") into its converse or a derived role ("is X needed to do Y?", "whom can one rely on when Y happens?") and both were judged weak because the quote states only who does Y best, not a necessity or a side effect; ask the mirror of the claim ("who does Y best?", "does X help with Y?") and do not invert it into a condition.
- The quote only says its subject acts "in some cases" without naming which, yet the generators asked the listener to name those cases ("Как понять, где нужно X?", "Когда пора перестать X?", "Где X действительно нужно?", "Когда X уже глупо?") and every one was judged weak with "в каких именно?" as the reason; when the quote gives a degree or frequency ("иногда", "в некоторых случаях", "обычно") with no criterion, ask only the "ли" questions that the degree answers ("всегда ли", "всё ли", "нужно ли ... всё подряд") and never ask when, where or how to tell.
- The filter removed both members of a duplicate pair of judged-direct questions ("Хватает ли одного опыта, чтобы работать хорошо?" and the same with "делать дело") and left no copy at all; when two questions share meaning and answer, keep exactly one (the higher score), never drop both.
- Scene questions widened the quote's subject to "люди" or "добро" in general ("Помогают ли люди без расчёта?") or asked from the receiver's side ("Нужно ли отплачивать?", "Нужно ли платить?") while the quote describes only the kind of person who gives, and all were judged weak; keep the quote's own kind of person as the subject of every scene question and never ask what the receiver owes.
- The core set `quoteProvidesIndicators` true for a quote that only places its subject between two named extremes, and the generators then asked "как отличить X от Y", "как понять, что ...", "где кончается X и начинается Y" and every one was judged weak because the quote gives no criterion for telling them apart; set the flag true only when the quote names an observable sign, and for a "middle between extremes" quote ask the "ли" questions about each extreme instead of a distinguishing sign.
- Obvious and scene questions were built from the "ибо" (reason) clause alone, with the quote's central subject dropped ("есть ли в смертном человеке бессмертное?", "как смертному прикоснуться к бессмертию?", "зачем людям рожать детей?", "что остаётся от человека в детях?"), and most were judged weak because the quote's main claim is about the subject and the reason is only its support; when a quote has a claim plus a reason clause, name the central subject in every question and use the reason clause only to enrich it, not as the question's topic.
- An obvious question used a word of the quote in its everyday economic sense ("Откуда у денег их ценность?" for a quote about the meaning of money) and was judged weak because that reading falls in the core's `forbiddenAnalogyDomains`; check obvious questions against `forbiddenAnalogyDomains` as well as scenes, and when a quote word has an economic or technical reading, phrase the question with "для мужчины", "ради" or a "ли" form that fixes the human sense.
- The quote states only a purpose ("not for X, but for Y"), yet obvious and scene questions asked whether the purpose is achieved or whether knowing is enough ("Стану ли я Y, если ...?", "Делает ли знание человека Y?", "Можно ли стать Y, только читая?") and all were judged weak because the quote never says the goal is reached or what suffices; for a purpose quote ask only about the purpose itself ("зачем", "ради чего", "ради X ли?") and do not ask about results or sufficiency.
- Scene questions dropped the domain the quote speaks in (poetry, fiction) and asked about the general idea ("правда", "история", "верят") with no domain word, and most were judged weak because the quote covers only that domain and the question pulled it into forbidden areas such as testimony and trust; keep the quote's own domain word (поэзия, вымысел, книга, фильм) in every scene question, not only its central subject.
- The quote is a diagnosis of the wrongdoer (what he needs, what he does), yet every scene question moved to the victim's side and asked what to do or whether one is to blame ("Можно ли успокоить ...?", "Можно ли защититься ...?", "Помогает ли безупречность ...?", "Виноват ли тот, кого ...?") and all were judged weak because the quote gives no advice; when the quote only describes the actor, keep the actor as the subject of every scene question and do not ask what the other side can do.
- The quote defines one concept through a wider one ("X is all of Y", "not-X is Y in general"), yet the obvious branch also asked "Что такое Y?" for the wider term and "какой Y самый ...?" for its opposite, and these were judged weak because the quote explains X, not Y, and ranks nothing; ask "что такое" only for the defined concept (and its named opposite), and phrase questions about the wider term as "ли" questions that the equation answers ("есть ли разница между X и Y?").
- Scene "почему/отчего" questions already named the cause in their wording ("Почему от долгого лежания человек слабеет?", "Отчего тело слабеет, если его не нагружать?") and every one was judged weak because the quote's claim was part of the question; when the quote is a plain claim, ask what the cause does or whether it harms ("Что делает с человеком X?", "Вредно ли X?"), never "почему" plus the cause plus its effect.
- Scene questions dropped the quote's qualifier ("продолжительное", "долгое") and asked about the bare thing ("Отдых без движения делает человека сильнее или слабее?") or stretched a word into another sense ("хватка", "ничего не делая"), and were judged weak because the quote covers only the qualified case; carry the qualifier and the quote's own noun into every scene question.
- For an observation quote whose core says only that the subject differs by context, the obvious branch also wrote open catalogue questions ("Какие бывают X?", "Что характерно для X?") and a "Где X остаётся тем же" question, and all were judged weak at the same low score because the quote names no kinds, traits or place; ask only the "ли" mirror questions about the stated dependence ("Зависит ли X от Y?", "Одинаков ли X при любом Y?") and skip open "какие/что характерно/где" forms.
- The quote defines its subject through a clause that names no content ("всё, внушающее смелость"), yet obvious and scene questions asked for that content ("Что помогает быть X?", "На что опереться?", "Где взять X?", "Что делает человека X?", "Откуда берётся X?") and all were judged weak because the answer is circular and the listener asks "а что именно?"; when a quote's second half only repeats its subject, ask the definition questions ("что значит", "что нужно, чтобы") and skip questions that request the unnamed content.
- The filter returned an empty `compact` list although ten questions were kept, so no short dialogue versions were produced; write a short version for every kept question, and treat an empty `compact` next to a non-empty kept list as a failure to fix before saving.
- The filter wrote a short version for only one of eleven kept questions, so `compact` was non-empty but nearly empty; write a short version for every kept question, and check that the count of `compact` entries equals the count of kept questions before saving.
- The filter wrote a short version for every kept question although all of them were already 3 to 9 words ("Одиночка ли человек по своей природе?", "Нужно ли человеку жить среди людей?"), following the lessons above that demand a short version for each kept question; the step rule skips a question that is already short and alive, so write a short version only for kept questions longer than nine words or windy, and the "compact count equals kept count" check applies only when the kept questions are long.
- The quote only observes how people behave (`quoteIsObservation` true, `quoteExplainsWhy` true, all how/principle flags false), yet obvious and scene questions asked for advice or permission ("Стоит ли думать о ...?", "Можно ли откладывать ...?", "Как жить, зная ...?", "Что толку ...?") and every one was judged weak because the quote prescribes nothing; for a behavioral observation ask only descriptive "ли" and "почему" questions about what people do and why, and never "стоит ли", "можно ли", "как жить".
- A superlative on one attribute ("самая точная") was restated as a different attribute or a general rank ("высшая из наук", "какое место среди наук") in history and obvious questions, and both were judged weak because the quote ranks its subject only by that one attribute; keep the quote's own attribute word in every question about a superlative.
