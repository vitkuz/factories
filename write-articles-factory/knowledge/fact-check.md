# Fact-checking articles

Check every article against its cited sources and the research notes. Open the
source URL when you can; a claim is only verified if the source actually says it.

## Per claim, one verdict
- **OK** — the source supports it as written.
- **WRONG** — the source says something else. Give the correct version.
- **UNSUPPORTED** — no source, or the source does not say it. Suggest cut or
  soften.
- **OVERSTATED** — directionally right but stronger than the evidence (e.g.
  "proves" for a correlation, a single study as consensus, stale numbers).
- **DEAD LINK** — the source cannot be reached; note whether another source in
  the research covers it.

## Report shape
- One section per article, named by its file.
- A table: claim (quoted short) · verdict · fix.
- Only non-OK claims need a row; end each section with the count checked and
  the count flagged.
- A final summary: total flagged, and which articles need changes.

You report; you never rewrite an article yourself.
