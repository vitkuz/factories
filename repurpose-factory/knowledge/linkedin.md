# LinkedIn post

- 150–300 words. One idea from the core, not all of them.
- First two lines are the hook: they show before "…see more". No greeting, no "I'm excited to share".
- Short paragraphs, 1–2 sentences, blank line between. At most one short list.
- One concrete detail (a number, a quote, a moment) from the core.
- End with a question or a clear takeaway, not a call to "like and share".
- At most 3 hashtags, at the end. No emoji walls; one or two at most, or none.
- Link to the source goes in a final line "Source: <url>" (people often move it to a comment).
- File: the post text, then a line `---`, then `Hook used:` and `Core points used:` (numbers from the core).
