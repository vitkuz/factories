# Blog post

- 800–1,400 words, Markdown. Front matter: `title`, `description` (≤ 155 characters), `slug`, `tags` (3–5).
- Title: specific and true to the thesis; no clickbait the body does not pay off.
- Opening paragraph states the thesis and why the reader should care.
- H2 sections follow the key points; merge small ones. Each section gives the reader something to do or check.
- Quote the source verbatim where the core gives a quote, with the speaker.
- Credit and link the source near the top ("Based on …") and again at the end.
- No facts beyond the core. If a section needs one, leave it out.
