# Repurpose package

One folder a person can post from without opening anything else.

## package.md
- A table: platform, file, word or character count, hook used, status (included / dropped by the person).
- Then each included piece in full, under its own heading, copied unchanged from its file.
- The source line: title, link, author.

## calendar.md
A suggested posting order over one week, one row per included platform: day, time window, platform, what to post, and why this order (for example: the blog first so the other posts can link to it; the newsletter after the blog; the thread and LinkedIn mid-week; the Short when the others have landed). Say it is a suggestion; there is no scheduling.

## Rules
- Copy pieces unchanged. Never edit, merge or improve them here.
- Leave out every platform the person dropped, and say so in the table.
