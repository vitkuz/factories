# Newsletter issue

- 300–600 words, Markdown, written to one reader ("you").
- Subject line: 3 options, each ≤ 50 characters, plus one preview text ≤ 90 characters.
- Open with why this landed in their inbox this week, in one or two sentences.
- The body: the thesis, the 3 most useful key points, one quote.
- A "Try this" section: one concrete action from the core.
- Close with the source link and one line inviting a reply.
- No facts beyond the core.
