# Shorts / Reels script (60 s vertical)

- 130–160 spoken words (about 60 seconds). Count them.
- Table with columns: `time` | `voice-over` | `on screen (text / visual)`.
- 0–2 s: the hook, spoken and as on-screen text. It must work with the sound off.
- One idea only, from the core. Short sentences written for the ear; no lists read aloud.
- Show a number or quote from the core on screen at least once.
- End with a payoff or a loop back to the hook, not "follow for more".
- After the table: a title (≤ 60 characters), a caption (≤ 150 characters), and 3 hashtags.
